SeaThrough privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

SeaThrough is an Android app by Mindylabs, Berlin, Germany ("we", "us"). It lets you set time limits for the apps you choose and floods an app with a calm sea when you overstay its limit. This policy explains what the app handles, why, and your rights. Contact: Theluckydude777@gmail.com.

The short version. No account, no ads, no analytics. SeaThrough reads which apps you use, on your phone, to apply the limits you set and to show you your own numbers. That information stays on your phone and we never receive it. Purchases go through Google Play.

What the app reads and keeps on your phone

  • App usage: which app is on screen and for how long, checked every 2 seconds while the guard runs, and daily totals for the apps you limit, for up to the last 7 days. Used to run your limits, the flood, the warnings and countdown, your own numbers (today, this week, days a year at this rate), the talking fish, the weekly summary and your time-back numbers.
  • Once during setup: 7 days of daily totals for every app you can open, to show which apps take most of your day and to personalise the plan screen. These totals are shown, not stored.
  • Apps on your phone: the apps you can open from your home screen (names and icons), read on your phone for the app picker and the golden fish. Only the apps you pick are saved.
  • Your settings and progress: the apps you chose and their limits, switches such as Riptide and the life ring, the golden fish's apps (up to 5), dry days and reef fish, and today's time per limited app.
  • Your subscription status, cached so the app works offline for a short time.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read. It is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, is never sent to us or to anyone else, and is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Tide cards you share

If you share your weekly Tide card, the app saves the image to your phone's Pictures/SeaThrough folder and opens Android's share sheet. The image shows your time numbers and a link to SeaThrough on Google Play. Where it goes is your choice. The image stays in your photos until you delete it, also after you uninstall the app.

Purchases

SeaThrough Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy); we never receive your card details. Google Play gives us order information (for example the product, date, country and order ID), which we use to handle refunds, support and our legal obligations, and keep for as long as tax law requires. Google Play's billing library, which the app uses to sell and check purchases, may send diagnostic information about purchases to Google under Google's policy. The app checks your subscription with Google Play on your phone.

Permissions and why

  • Usage access: to know which app is open and for how long. Read on your phone only.
  • Display over other apps: to draw the sea over an app you limited. The app does not read what is on your screen.
  • Notifications: the guard notification, the warnings and countdown before a flood, Riptide's "Let me in", the golden fish's "Swim to" action, the Sunday weekly summary and the reminder 2 days before a trial ends. The reminders are scheduled with Android alarms on your phone, which needs no extra permission.
  • Foreground service and starting at boot: to keep the guard running while you use other apps, and after a restart if you had it on and have an app limited.
  • Internet and billing: used only by Google Play Billing for purchases.

SeaThrough does not use an Accessibility service and does not read the content of other apps, your messages, photos, files, contacts or location.

Children

SeaThrough is made for adults (18 and over) changing their own habits. It is not directed at children and is not a parental-control or monitoring app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. We hold no usage data: it is on your phone, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. For purchase records, write to Theluckydude777@gmail.com. You can revoke Usage access, Display over other apps or notifications at any time in Android settings, and stop the guard from its notification. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit).

Legal basis (GDPR): processing on your phone provides the app you asked for (Art. 6(1)(b)); order information is processed to fulfil the purchase and our legal obligations (Art. 6(1)(b) and (c)).

Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app's release notes.
